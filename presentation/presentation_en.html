Engineering Decisions

3 Core Architectural Trade-Offs

1. Native ElevenLabs Agent vs. Cascaded Pipeline

Cascaded pipelines (STT→LLM→TTS) exceed 2.0s latency. Native loop achieves ~430ms. For seniors, latency directly equals trust.

2. Eleven v3 Conversational vs. Flash Model

Flash is fast (75ms) but sounds robotic. Eleven v3 (~280ms) delivers emotional warmth, prosodic nuance, and empathy.

3. Turn Timeout 3.0s & Patient Mode

Default 1.0–1.5s cuts off seniors mid-sentence. 3.0s timeout with patient listening accommodates natural speech pauses.

Every choice was an intentional trade-off between pure benchmark speed vs. domain-specific guest conversion.
Data & Retrieval Strategy

Knowledge Base (RAG) — Zero Hallucination Policy

Vector RAG Architecture

  • 5 Curated Markdown Files: Access, Treatments, Rooms, Dining, Policies.
  • Prompt Mode: Pricing and emergency rules permanently loaded in context.
  • Auto Mode: Semantic chunking for treatment catalogs and driving directions.
  • Scalability: Scales effortlessly to hundreds of resort documents.

Balneological Safety Protocol

  • Mandatory Screening: Heart conditions and acute inflammation checks before thermal baths.
  • Strict Grounding: Missing info triggers immediate warm transfer — never hallucinate.
  • Risk Management: Hallucinated price = sales issue; Hallucinated medical advice = legal liability.
Highlight safety and compliance. In medical wellness, a hallucinating AI is worse than no AI.

Data Governance & Compliance

Enterprise Security, Healthcare Privacy & GDPR

Security Architecture

  • Zero Retention Mode (ZRM): Audio streams and transcripts exist strictly in volatile RAM — zero disk storage.
  • PII Redaction: Automated masking for national ID, phone numbers, and health records in logs.
  • EU Data Residency: Compute, LLM inference, and vector embeddings located strictly within EU (Frankfurt / Dublin).
  • Signed URLs: Frontend connects securely via 15-min TTL tokens without exposing server xi-api-key.
For healthcare and resort management, compliance and security are non-negotiable requirements.
Multi-Tenant Architecture

Multi-Hotel Scaling: 1 Master Agent, 10 Properties

Sub-Linear Scaling Model

  • The Challenge: 10 properties typically means 10 separate agents, 10 siloed knowledge bases, and linear maintenance costs.
  • Centralized Master Core: 1 Master Agent with dynamic variables (hotel_name, location, phone, pms_id).
  • SIP UUI (RFC 7433): Context metadata injected dynamically inside the telephony header per inbound number.
  • Zero Duplication: Adding a new hotel requires updating metadata in PMS — zero prompt duplication.
Key commercial argument — ROI across 10 properties scales sub-linearly.
